Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Our Service

How long do meals stay fresh in the fridge?

Our meals are prepared fresh and packaged in sealed, airtight containers that maintain optimal quality and taste for 4–5 days refrigerated.

How do I heat up the meals?

Pop the container lid slightly open and microwave on high for 2 to 3 minutes, or transfer to an oven-safe dish at 350°F (175°C) for 10–12 minutes.

What if I miss the Friday or Monday noon cutoff?

Orders submitted after the cutoff time are automatically assigned to the following eligible batch date, ensuring your meals are always prepared fresh.

Can I pick up my order in person?

Yes! Free store pickup is available at George the Greek, 3575 Lake Shore Blvd W in Etobicoke. Select "Store Pickup" at checkout.
