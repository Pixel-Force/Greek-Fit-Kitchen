Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · Managed by George the Greek

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or create an account with Greek Fit Kitchen, we collect your name, email address, mobile phone number, delivery address, and meal customization selections. We do not store raw credit card numbers or security CVV codes on our servers.

2. How Your Information is Used

Your personal information is used exclusively to:

  • Fulfill and deliver your scheduled Sunday or Wednesday meal batches.
  • Send transactional order confirmation receipts and delivery SMS alerts.
  • Calculate precise driving delivery fees ($5.99 base + $0.99/km) from 3575 Lake Shore Blvd W, Etobicoke.
  • Allow 1-click reordering in your customer account dashboard.

3. Payment Security & Tokenization

Online payment transactions are tokenized and processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant credit card gateways. Secret API keys remain server-side and are never exposed to client browsers.

4. No Unauthorized Sharing or Selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketing firms. Customer data is only shared with our operational delivery couriers and kitchen preparation staff as necessary to complete your order.

5. Contact Our Privacy Officer

For questions or to request deletion of your saved customer profile, email: orders@greekfitkitchen.ca or visit our physical partner station at George the Greek, 3575 Lake Shore Blvd W, Etobicoke, ON M8W 1P5.
