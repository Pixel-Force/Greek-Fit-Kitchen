Our Story & Heritage

ABOUT GREEK FIT KITCHEN

Founded on the belief that healthy eating should never mean dry, bland chicken and tasteless rice. We bring authentic Aegean tavern culinary depth to modern meal preparation.

The Origin

Born from George the Greek in South Etobicoke

For years, George the Greek (3575 Lake Shore Blvd W, Etobicoke) has served authentic Mediterranean comfort food — souvlaki skewered by hand, chicken flame-grilled with wild oregano, and lemon roasted potatoes steeped in aromatic citrus jus.

Fitness enthusiasts and busy professionals kept asking: "Can you prepare our weekly food so we don't have to cook?"

In partnership with Lean Meal (leanmeal.ca), we engineered Greek Fit Kitchen: the exact same authentic culinary recipes, prepared in clean scheduled batches with balanced macros, packaged airtight, and delivered straight to your door.

Our Standards

The Greek Fit Kitchen Difference

Virgin Olive Oil & Herbs

We reject cheap canola or seed oils. Our marinades are built with pure extra virgin olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and wild Greek oregano.
