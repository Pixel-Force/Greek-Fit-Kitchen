Refund & Cancellation Policy

At Greek Fit Kitchen, we are committed to delivering fresh, healthy Mediterranean meals prepared to the highest standard. Review our guidelines for cancellations, adjustments, and quality guarantees below.

1. Advance Preparation & Cancellation Window

To ensure peak freshness and zero food waste, every meal is prepared to order using local Ontario ingredients. Orders must be submitted before our strict batch cutoffs:

  • Sunday Delivery / Pickup: Order cutoff is Friday at 12:00 PM EST. Cancellations submitted prior to this time receive a 100% full refund back to the original payment card.
  • Wednesday Delivery / Pickup: Order cutoff is Monday at 12:00 PM EST. Cancellations submitted prior to this time receive a 100% full refund.

2. Late Cancellations & Perishable Goods Policy

Because raw proteins and seasonal produce are specifically procured and prepped immediately following the cutoff deadline, orders cannot be cancelled, modified, or refunded once the batch cutoff has passed. In accordance with Ontario health and commercial food safety regulations, perishable cooked items cannot be restocked or resold once packaging and dispatch preparation has commenced.

3. Freshness & Cold-Chain Quality Guarantee

We guarantee that all meals leave our commercial kitchen at compliant cold storage temperatures (below 4°C / 40°F) in insulated thermal packaging with food-grade gel refrigerant packs.

If your delivery arrives damaged, compromised, or missing an item, please notify us within 24 hours of delivery receipt. Include your Order ID and clear digital photos of the damaged container or seal. Upon verification, we will promptly issue an instant replacement meal in the subsequent cycle, store credit, or a direct itemized refund.

4. Delivery Access & Unattended Deliveries

Customers are responsible for providing correct buzzer codes, concierge instructions, or safe porch drop-off locations. If a courier cannot access the property and cannot reach the customer via the provided phone number within the scheduled delivery window, meals will be left at the most secure exterior location. Refunds cannot be provided for meals spoiled or stolen due to incorrect customer-supplied delivery instructions or unattended porch drops beyond recommended cooling times.

5. Refund Processing Times

Approved refunds are processed through our payment gateway (Stripe) within 1 to 2 business days. Depending on your financial institution or card issuer, funds will appear back on your original statement within 3 to 7 business days.
