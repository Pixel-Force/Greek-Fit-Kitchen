ABOUT GREEK FIT KITCHEN

Big Greek Flavour Meets Modern Meal Prep

Greek Fit Kitchen was established to bridge the gap between delicious Greek tavern cuisine and disciplined weekly meal preparation.

Managed by George the Greek at 3575 Lake Shore Blvd W in Etobicoke and powered by Lean Meal (leanmeal.ca), we prepare our marinades with extra virgin olive oil, mountain oregano, fresh garlic, and citrus. No shortcuts, no greasy takeaways — just clean, protein-packed fuel.
