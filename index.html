HOW GREEK FIT KITCHEN WORKS

We operate as an advance scheduled meal-preparation business, ensuring zero food waste and maximum freshness for every customer.

1

Select Your Meals

Choose a minimum of 6 meals from our 20 healthy Greek dishes. Order 7+ to get 10% OFF your entire meal prep order.

2

Choose 2 Sides Free

Any plate comes with 2 included sides (lemon potatoes, rice pilaf, sautéed veggies, etc.), except Pasta al Greco.

3

Schedule Delivery / Pickup

Pick Sunday or Wednesday. Sunday orders cutoff Friday 12pm; Wednesday cutoff Monday 12pm. Pickup at George the Greek is always free.

4

Heat, Eat & Thrive

Arrives fresh and refrigerated. Ready in just 2 minutes in the microwave. Healthy, delicious Greek fuel with zero dish cleanup!
